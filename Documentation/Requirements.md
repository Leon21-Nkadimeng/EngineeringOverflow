### \### Functional Requirements

### \#### Authentication

### \- users must be able to register an account.

### \- users must be able to login and logout.

### \- users must be able to reset their password if they forget it

### \- users must be able to reset their password while logged in.

### \- users must be able to update their email.

### \- users must be able to deactivate their account while logged in.

### \- The system must securely hash user passwords before storing them in the database.

### \- The system must verify email addresses when they're changed.

### 

### \#### Profile requirements

### \- The system must validate usernames to ensure that they are unique.

### \- 

### \- users can provide their educational experience when editing their profile

### \- users can change their profile details (profile image, their bio, public username, date of birth, location, links to other social profiles, and educational experience)
