### Problem
Engineering students often struggle with difficult concepts and coursework but lack a dedicated platform where they can easily ask questions, share knowledge, access useful learning resources, and learn from the experiences of other engineering students. Existing platforms are either too general or focused primarily on specific subjects, leaving a gap for a community specifically designed around the needs of engineering students. This can leave struggling students isolated and unsure where to find reliable guidance or support.

## Solution
EngineerOVerflow will be a dedicated online community platform for engineering students to ask questions, share knowledge, access educational resource, and learn from the experiences of their peers. The platform will allow students to create profiles based on their educational background, ask and answer questions, participate in discussions, share useful content, provide feedback on contributions through voting and other community features.
